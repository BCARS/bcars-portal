Grant access

The account must already exist. Granting is the decision that this login may read this record, and nothing else follows from it.

Create an account

Creates a member login for an email address, or reuses the one that address already has. It grants no access to any record on its own, and it sets no password — the member chooses their own.

Send a sign-in link

Emails the member a link to set their password. Sent the same way whether or not the address has an account, so this cannot be used to check who is registered.
